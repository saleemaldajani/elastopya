In conclusion, this paper has covered various aspects of investigating thermally-induced spontaneous demixing and embrittlement. By considering parameters such as stable spinodal compositions, elastic moduli, diffusion length, and embrittlement criteria, we can deepen our understanding of material behavior and stability. The proposed method offers scalability to high-throughput datasets and provides a valuable tool for materials science and engineering research.

The experimental results presented in this chapter provide valuable insights into the phenomenon of plastic embrittlement and its connection to quantum elastodynamics. The thermally-induced spontaneous demixing, elastic anisotropy, and stiffening analyses contribute to a better understanding of material behavior and pave the way for future advancements in material design and industrial processes.
