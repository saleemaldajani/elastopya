# Captions for the strip versions (panel c)

Panels (a) and (b) are unchanged from the manuscript captions. Add the panel (c) sentence below.

**Fig. 1 (`fig1_free_energy_strip`)**
(a) Demixing free energy $\Delta G_{\mathrm{demix}} = -\Delta G_{\mathrm{mix}}$ (Eq. 1) versus chromium
composition $x_{\mathrm{Cr}}$: muffin-tin approximation, GGA (present VASP calculations), experiment,
and the PWSCF–PAW and SIESTA–NC interaction models of Levesque et al. at 298.15 K. (b) Curvature of
the PWSCF–PAW demixing free energy: the exact analytic second derivative of the model (solid line),
three-point second differences of its tabulated values (filled circles), and two-point differences
with a quadratic fit (open circles, dashed). Shading marks positive demixing curvature, i.e., the
spinodally unstable compositions. **(c) One (001) atomic plane of fully decomposed ($\phi = 1$) MD
structures (25³ bcc cells, Bonny EAM potential) at the indicated Cr contents; red, Fe; blue, Cr.
Inside the spinodal window the alloy separates into interpenetrating Fe-rich $\alpha$ and Cr-rich
$\alpha'$ domains, whereas at the dilute ends (10 and 95% Cr) the minority element forms only
isolated clusters.**

**Fig. 2 (`fig2_elastic_mapping_strip`, `fig2_elastic_mapping_orientation_violin_bulk_strip`)**
(a), (b) as in the manuscript. **(c) One (001) atomic plane of random bcc Fe–Cr solid solutions at
the indicated Cr contents (red, Fe; blue, Cr). Every elastic tensor in (a) and (b) describes such a
homogeneous, chemically disordered alloy; none contains the $\alpha/\alpha'$ domains of Fig. 1c.**

**Fig. 3 (`fig3_fsaw_strip`, and the `_relative`, `_mddecomp`, `_alpha`, `_crcolor` variants)**
(a), (b) as in the manuscript. **(c) One (001) atomic plane of the 25³ MD cell of Fe$_{64}$Cr$_{36}$
at spinodal conversion fractions $\phi$ = 0.5 (random alloy) to 1.0 (fully separated); red, Fe;
blue, Cr. Fe-rich $\alpha$ and Cr-rich $\alpha'$ domains form and coarsen as $\phi$ increases;
these are the structures whose SAW frequencies are plotted in (b).**
